02What the engine does

Beyond the arithmetic.

The rate is the easy part. Everything that makes a return defensible sits around it.

+

Adjustment library

Define your add-backs and deductions once — entertainment, depreciation differences, provisions, interest limitation — and apply them consistently every period. Each is versioned, so a prior year keeps the rules that were in force when it was filed.

+

Relief elections, made explicitly

Small Business Relief, free zone qualifying income and group relief are positions you take deliberately. The engine records who elected what, on what date, and on what basis.

+

Working papers attached

Support hangs off the computation line it justifies rather than sitting in a folder named after the month. When a question arrives later, the answer is where the number is.
