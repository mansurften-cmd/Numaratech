## 6. Checked and clean

- **Security headers:** CSP `script-src 'self'` with no inline, HSTS preload, nosniff, frame-ancestors none, COOP, Referrer-Policy; HTML revalidates, hashed assets and fonts immutable.
- **Third parties:** none at runtime. No cookies. Fonts self-hosted and subset. Only external origin is Web3Forms, allowed in `connect-src`/`form-action`.
- **Secrets:** none tracked. `wrangler.toml` holds no account id or token.
- **Weight:** 20 HTML pages (largest 26 KB), 43 KB CSS, 6.6 KB JS in four files, 76 KB fonts. OG cards 660 KB, fetched only by crawlers.
- **Pre-launch indexing block:** `Disallow: /` and `X-Robots-Tag` still in place — correct on workers.dev. `LAUNCH.md` is the sequence to lift it.
- **Calculators:** CT calculator server-rendered == post-JS on every output; 2,000,000 + 100,000 → 2,100,000 / 155,250. Home console ↔ estimator one computation. Nothing leaves the browser.
- **Print:** both calculators print without site chrome.
- **Mobile:** 20 routes × 8 viewports, no overflow, no clipped text. Tablet nav at 14 widths.
