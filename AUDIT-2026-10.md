## 11. Association with G R A M P Network, 25 October 2026

Added at the owner's request: a short "Association" section on `/about/` and
a line in the footer of every page, both reading from `SITE.association`.

The wording is deliberately narrow, and rests on the network's own
correspondence rather than on its website (which the build environment
cannot reach):

- **Name** written "G R A M P Network", with spaces and no logo — the
  founder member firm's instruction of 18 October 2026.
- **Relationship** "associated with", the owner's word. The site states
  that NUMARATECH is a separate business and **not a member firm**: on
  24 October 2026 the founder member firm asked for the UAE presence to be
  listed on gramp.in under Gautam Joshi & Co. LLP, "not as UAE member".
- **Description** "a network of Chartered Accountant firms with offices
  across India", from the network's signature block (network offices in nine
  Indian states).
- No structured-data claim: schema.org has no "associated with", and
  `memberOf` would assert membership.

**Owner:** confirm this wording with the network before launch. Indian CA
networks are governed by ICAI's networking guidelines, which cover how a
network's name may be used; strengthening it to "member", "partner" or
"part of" should only follow the network's written agreement.
